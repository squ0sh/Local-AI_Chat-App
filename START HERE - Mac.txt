CAPSULE — macOS INTEL (x64)

1. Extract the entire ZIP into a writable folder.
2. Open the Capsule folder in Finder.
3. Double-click Local AI Chat.command.

Terminal opens automatically to run Capsule; you do not need to type commands.
Keep that window open. Your browser opens when Capsule is ready; otherwise
visit http://127.0.0.1:5173.

Capsule is not Apple-notarized. Gatekeeper may block the downloaded launcher
or runtime. Capsule's Ed25519 signature is NOT an Apple Developer ID signature.
If blocked, stop and consult the person who shared it and Apple's guidance:
https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445
Do not disable Gatekeeper, remove quarantine attributes, or bypass a malware
warning. Managed Macs may not permit this software. A seamless first launch
cannot currently be promised.

Choose a model in Model library and approve its download. Keep the .portable
folder private: it contains models, chats and settings. More help is in
FRIENDS-AND-FAMILY.md; trust limitations are in SECURITY.md.
